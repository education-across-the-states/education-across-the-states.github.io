[NH] NH Education Trends in Southern NH — October 23, 2026: HB 1817 and the Free Seat

October 25, 2026 · 47:49

▶ Play episode
Episode notes & sources

Alex and Jordan devote the full hour to House Bill 1817, the 2026 law giving resident Education Freedom Account students free seats in their district classes, clubs, teams, and career and technical programs while the state sends districts no funding for those seats, and trace what that means for Manchester, Nashua, Derry, Salem, and the southern New Hampshire corridor in its first fall.
