Selected Investigations & Productions

One in Five: IEPs and 504 Plans in America

Investigation · October 2026

Analysis of the federal Civil Rights Data Collection (2023–24) and IDEA Section 618 child counts: why 19% of public school students now hold an IEP or a Section 504 plan, tested category by category against the instructional-casualty hypothesis. Published with full charts and sourcing at education-across-the-states.github.io/one-in-five/.

The Plan Nobody Audits — Section 504

National special · October 2026

Section 504-only identification has almost quadrupled in fifteen years under an unchanged legal test. Every on-air claim proof-checked against its source before publication.

Nashua's Facilities Crossroads

Special · October 2026

Coverage of the Harriman facilities master plan and the November school-closure vote, built from the public record, with on-record responses from a former Board of Education member aired verbatim.

Section-by-Section Law Series

Ongoing series

Plain-language, section-by-section walkthroughs of the laws that run schools — the federal IDEA statute (§1400–§1482) and New Hampshire's Ed 1100 rules — written for the teachers who work under them daily.

Production Standards

  • Sources published with every episode — documents, datasets, and reporting named for independent verification.

  • Right of reply for every institution and official covered; embargoes honored.
