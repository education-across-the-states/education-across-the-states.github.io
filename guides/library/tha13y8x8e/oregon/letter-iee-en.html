Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) Request Letter — Oregon

Free download from Education Across the States — companion to the Oregon IEP Guide for Parents, Step 4. Fill in the brackets, keep a dated copy, and send it so you have proof of the date.

[Your full name] [Your address] [Your phone] · [Your email]

[Date]

[Special Education Director's name] [School District name] [Address]

Re: Request for an Independent Educational Evaluation at public expense Student: [Child's full name], date of birth [DOB], grade [grade], [school name]

Dear [Name]:

I am writing to request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) of my child at public expense, under ORS 343.173(2) and OAR 581-015-2305, because I disagree with the district's evaluation [obtained / completed on (date)], specifically [the evaluation of (area — e.g., "the district's psychoeducational evaluation dated ___")].

Under Oregon's rules (OAR 581-015-2305), I understand that:

  • Upon this request, the district must, without unnecessary delay, either file a due process complaint to request a hearing to show that its evaluation is appropriate, or ensure that an independent educational evaluation is provided at public expense (OAR 581-015-2305(4)). Oregon's rule sets no number of days; I am asking the district to act promptly, and I will treat unexplained delay as unnecessary delay.
  • The district must provide me, on request, information about where an independent educational evaluation may be obtained and the district criteria applicable to IEEs (OAR 581-015-2305(2); ORS 343.173(4)).
  • The district's IEE criteria must be the same criteria the district uses when it initiates an evaluation (OAR 581-015-2305(3)(a)); beyond applicable criteria, the district may not impose conditions or timelines related to obtaining an IEE at public expense (OAR 581-015-2305(3)(c)).
  • I am entitled to only one IEE at public expense each time the district conducts an evaluation with which I disagree (OAR 581-015-2305(1)(b)); this request concerns the district evaluation identified above.
  • The results of the IEE must be considered by the district in any decision regarding the provision of a free appropriate public education to my child (OAR 581-015-2305(7)).

I am not required to explain why I disagree with the district's evaluation, and I understand the district may not use a request for an explanation to delay either providing the IEE or filing for due process (OAR 581-015-2305(6)).

Please send me the district's IEE criteria and information about obtaining an IEE, and let me know how the district will proceed. I can be reached at [phone/email].

Thank you.

Sincerely, [Your signature] [Your printed name]

Before you send — checklist

  • ☐ The district evaluation you disagree with is identified (evaluator/area and date) — your right attaches to that evaluation
  • ☐ Letter dated; your copy kept; sent with proof of the date
  • ☐ Calendar note: Oregon's standard is "without unnecessary delay" — there is no day-count. If weeks pass with neither a funded IEE nor a due process filing by the district, write again citing OAR 581-015-2305(4), and see Steps 13–15 of the Oregon IEP Guide (mediation, state complaint, due process)
  • ☐ If your chosen examiner falls outside the district's criteria: you have the opportunity to demonstrate that unique circumstances justify the exception (OAR 581-015-2305(3)(d)) — put that showing in writing
  • ☐ An IEE you obtain on your own must also be considered by the team — send it in writing and ask that it be addressed at the next IEP meeting

General information, not legal advice. Oregon rules stated are from ORS 343.173 and OAR 581-015-2305 (verified November 5, 2026). Standing correction: some template letters elsewhere assert a fixed number of days for a district to respond to an IEE request. Oregon law contains no such deadline — "without unnecessary delay" is the complete and correct standard.
