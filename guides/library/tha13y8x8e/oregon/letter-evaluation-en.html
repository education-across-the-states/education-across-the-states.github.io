Evaluation Request Letter — Oregon

Free download from Education Across the States — companion to the Oregon IEP Guide for Parents. Fill in the brackets, keep a dated copy, and send it so you have proof of the date (email, or hand-deliver and ask for a date-stamped copy).

[Your full name] [Your address] [Your phone] · [Your email]

[Date]

[Principal's name / Special Education Director's name] [School name] [School District name] [Address]

Re: Request for special education evaluation Student: [Child's full name], date of birth [DOB], grade [grade], [school name]

Dear [Name]:

I am writing to request that my child be evaluated to determine eligibility for special education and related services under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) and Oregon law (ORS Chapter 343; OAR 581-015-2080, child find). I suspect my child has a disability affecting [his/her/their] education, in the following areas:

[Describe what you observe, in plain words — for example: "reading — still cannot decode simple words and avoids reading aloud; attention — cannot sustain focus through a 15-minute task; speech — difficult for unfamiliar listeners to understand; behavior — frequent meltdowns during transitions, sent home early four times this month."]

I understand that under Oregon rules:

  • Evaluation planning will be conducted by the team, including me, reviewing existing information — including the information I provide (OAR 581-015-2115). I am available to participate and to share records.
  • No evaluation will occur until the district provides prior written notice and obtains my informed written consent (OAR 581-015-2090). Please send me the consent form promptly so the timeline can begin.
  • Once I give written consent, Oregon requires the evaluation to be completed and the eligibility determination made within 60 school days of my consent (OAR 581-015-2110(5)).
  • The evaluation must cover all areas related to the suspected disability, including, as appropriate: health, vision, hearing, social and emotional status, general intelligence, academic performance, communicative status, and motor abilities (OAR 581-015-2110).

Please also consider my child under Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act if the team determines an IEP may not be appropriate.

Please contact me at [phone/email] to schedule the evaluation-planning discussion and to provide the consent form. I will keep a copy of this letter for my records.

Thank you for your attention to this request.

Sincerely, [Your signature] [Your printed name]

Before you send — checklist

  • ☐ Areas of concern listed in your own words (the evaluation must address each suspected area)
  • ☐ Letter dated; your copy kept
  • ☐ Sent by email or hand-delivered with a date stamp requested — the request date matters even though Oregon's clock starts at consent
  • ☐ Calendar note: if no consent form arrives within a reasonable time, write again and note the delay — the 60-school-day clock cannot start until the district gives you the form and you sign it
  • ☐ When you sign consent: write the date down — the eligibility meeting is due within 60 school days of it

General information, not legal advice. Oregon timelines stated are from OAR 581-015-2090, 581-015-2110, and 581-015-2115 (verified November 5, 2026).
