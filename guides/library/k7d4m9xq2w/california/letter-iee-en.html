Request for an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at Public Expense — California

Use this letter when you disagree with an evaluation the district performed and want an independent evaluator — paid for by the district. Fill in the [brackets], and keep a dated copy.

[Your full name] [Your address] [Your phone] · [Your email]

[Date]

[Name of special education director] [District name] [Address]

Re: Request for Independent Educational Evaluation at public expense Student: [Child's full name], Date of birth: [DOB], Grade: [grade], School: [school]

Dear [Name]:

I am the parent/guardian of [Child's name]. The district completed an evaluation of my child, specifically: [name the evaluation and its date — e.g., "the psychoeducational evaluation dated March 3, 2026" / "the speech and language assessment completed in February 2026"].

I disagree with that evaluation. Under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act and its regulations (34 CFR 300.502) and California Education Code §56329, I am requesting an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at public expense — that is, an evaluation by a qualified examiner who is not employed by the district, paid for by the district.

I understand the district's options under the law: to fund the IEE, or to file a due process complaint to demonstrate that its own evaluation was appropriate. I also understand that federal law requires the district to act without unnecessary delay. Please note that the law sets no specific number of days for this — I am not asserting a fixed deadline, and I am asking the district not to substitute one either; I am asking for prompt action without unnecessary delay, and for a written response telling me which course the district will take.

Please also send me:

  1. The district's criteria for IEEs (including any criteria for the location of the evaluation and the qualifications of the examiner), which I understand must be the same criteria the district uses for its own evaluations; and
  2. Information about where and how to obtain an IEE, which the district is required to provide on request.

[Optional: I have identified a qualified independent examiner — [name, credentials, contact] — and request the district's approval to proceed with that examiner.]

Thank you. I look forward to your written response.

Sincerely,

[Your signature] [Your printed name]

Before you send — checklist

  • ☐ The district evaluation you disagree with is named, with its date (you get one IEE at public expense for each district evaluation you disagree with — name the right one)
  • ☐ The letter is dated and you kept a copy, with a note of how it was sent
  • ☐ You did not put a number of days in the letter — the legal standard is "without unnecessary delay," and invented deadlines (some template letters claim a "5-day rule") do not exist and can undermine an otherwise strong request
  • ☐ If weeks pass with no funding and no due process filing: that delay is itself the issue — escalate using the dispute steps in Part 5 of The California IEP Guide for Parents (state complaint to CDE is one option)
