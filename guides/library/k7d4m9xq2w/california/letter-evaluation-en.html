Request for Special Education Evaluation (Assessment) — California

Fill in the [brackets], print or email it, and keep a dated copy for yourself. Email is fine — it creates its own paper trail.

[Your full name] [Your address] [Your phone] · [Your email]

[Date]

[Name of principal or special education director] [School name / District name] [Address]

Re: Request for assessment for special education eligibility Student: [Child's full name], Date of birth: [DOB], Grade: [grade], School: [school]

Dear [Name]:

I am the parent/guardian of [Child's name]. I am writing to request that the district assess [Child's name] to determine eligibility for special education and related services under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act and California Education Code Part 30. I am requesting assessment in all areas of suspected disability, including:

  • [Area — e.g., reading and written expression: describe what you see in one or two sentences]
  • [Area — e.g., speech and language: …]
  • [Area — e.g., attention, behavior, social development, motor skills, health: …]

[Optional: I have attached (records/report cards/a letter from [doctor/therapist]) that describe my concerns.]

I understand that under California Education Code §56321(a), the district will provide me a proposed assessment plan in writing within 15 days of this referral, and that no assessment will begin until I have had the opportunity to consider and consent to that plan. I also understand that once I return written consent to the assessment plan, California law (Education Code §56344) requires the assessment to be completed and an IEP developed within 60 days.

Please send the proposed assessment plan to me at the address/email above. If you have any questions, or if the district declines any part of this request, please provide that decision and its reasons to me in writing.

Thank you for your attention to [Child's name]'s education.

Sincerely,

[Your signature] [Your printed name]

Before you send — checklist

  • ☐ Every area of concern is named (the assessment plan can only cover areas someone raised)
  • ☐ The letter is dated
  • ☐ You kept a copy (photo or scan is fine) and noted how and when you sent it
  • ☐ Calendar marked: assessment plan due within 15 days (not counting breaks between school terms or vacation days beyond five schooldays)
  • ☐ When the plan arrives: you have at least 15 days to decide — read it fully before signing; consent to assess is not consent to services or placement
