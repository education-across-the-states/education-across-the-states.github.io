Report for Education Across the States — From Where You Live

The go to place for any issue having to do with education across the nation

What it is

Education Across the States runs local internships across the country for aspiring journalists. Interns cover education where they live: school board and public meetings, in-person interviews, and research from primary sources — the reporting a national show can't do from a desk.

What you get

Published bylines on a national platform when your vetted work airs. Training in public-records and education-beat reporting. Real supervision and feedback on every submission. A portfolio of broadcast-standard work, and a completion letter from the producer at the end of a successful term.

What we require

Everything you produce is submitted to the producer for vetting — every claim checked against its source — before anything airs. Interviews are recorded only with clear consent. Nothing is ever invented: no quotes, no figures, no exceptions. Terms run one semester (12–14 weeks), hours are flexible and school-first, and the internship is unpaid and educational in structure, designed to pair with academic credit where your school offers it.

How to apply

Email educationacrossthestates@gmail.com with your name, your city and state, your school or background, two writing or audio samples (class work is fine), one local education story idea from your community, and your availability. If it looks like a fit, the next step is a short call with the producer, Charles Mayfield.
