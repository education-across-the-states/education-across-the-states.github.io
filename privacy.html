Privacy & How We Protect Your Information

The go to place for any issue having to do with education across the nation

Education Across the States is produced by Professional Development Inc. Producer: Charles Mayfield. This page explains what personal information our forms collect, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

Through our internship application form: your name and email, school and program, a résumé and optional writing sample, a copy of your state-issued ID (driver's license or state ID card), and — for applicants under 18 — a parent or guardian's name, email, and phone number. Through our call-request form: your name, email, phone number, what you'd like to discuss, and your availability.

Why we collect it

Only to evaluate internship applications, verify an applicant's age and identity, obtain a parent's or guardian's permission for applicants under 18, and schedule and prepare production calls. We use it for nothing else.

How we protect it

Submissions are stored in the production team's access-controlled Google account, and uploaded files go to that account's private Drive storage. Access is limited to the production team. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers. We share information only where the program requires it — for example, a parent's phone number is given to the team member who makes the permission call, and service providers that support our operations (such as the service that places production-team calls) receive only what they need to do that work.

ID copies

A state-issued ID copy is used only to verify age and identity, and is deleted once verification is complete.

Parental permission calls

For applicants under 18, we call the parent or guardian to confirm permission. That call is recorded with the parent's knowledge, and the recording is kept with the application record as our proof of permission.

How long we keep it

Application materials are kept while an application is active and for a short period afterward, then deleted. A call request is initiated by you and becomes the property of the show; call requests are retained indefinitely as production records.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to correct or delete your information: write to educationacrossthestates@gmail.com. We will act on the request promptly.
